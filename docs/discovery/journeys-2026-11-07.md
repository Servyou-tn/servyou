### What protects every write (live, 2026-11-06)

- **RLS on all 30 public tables.** No anon role can write anywhere.
- **Every client-writable table runs `enforce_active_user`**: a suspended account cannot write.
- **Server-only tables (no client policy):** `contact_messages`, `password_change_attempts`, `uploaded_objects`,
  `order_events`. Only the server writes them, or a trigger for `order_events`.
- **Storage:** no client INSERT / UPDATE policy on any bucket.
  - Every upload goes through a server action with the service role, plus an `uploaded_objects` provenance row.
  - Provenance triggers on `profiles`, `shops`, `product_images` and `freelancer_portfolio_items` refuse a file the
    account did not upload.
- **Locked columns are column-granted:**
  - `orders` UPDATE: only `status`, `cancelled_by`, `cancellation_reason`, `carrier`, `tracking_number`;
  - `profiles` UPDATE: only `full_name`, `city`, `language`, `phone`, `avatar_url`, `seller_type`;
  - `job_posts`, `shops`, `freelancer_profiles`, `data_exports`, `deletion_requests`, `disputes` and `reports` are
    column-granted too.
- **Order lifecycle (`check_order_status_transition`):**
  - Product: pending → accepted → prepared → dispatched → in_delivery → arrived, seller only.
  - Service: pending → accepted → arrived, seller only.
  - arrived → received: buyer only.
  - Cancel: either party, `cancelled_by` must match the caller's role. A reason is required after dispatch (product) or
    after acceptance (service).
  - A buyer can never cancel a service order (`enforce_buyer_cancel_product_only`).
  - received and cancelled are final.
  - On insert: status forced to pending, the database's clock, the seller and price from the item
    (`enforce_order_insert_integrity`, `set_order_snapshot`). No self-order, stock checked, a suspended seller's item is
    refused.
- **Phone reveal (`get_contact_phone`, SECURITY DEFINER):**
  - The seller sees the buyer's phone from « pending » on.
  - The buyer sees the seller's phone only once the order is accepted. A cancelled order keeps the reveal only if it
    had been accepted.
  - A mission owner sees the phone of a freelancer who proposed. The freelancer never sees the client's phone through
    a mission.
  - Nothing is revealed if either party is suspended.
  - Anon gets `null`.
- **Missions (`enforce_job_post_writes`):**
  - A post is always created open.
  - The owner can only move it open ↔ filled within 30 days, or delete it.
  - Its content locks once it has a proposal.
  - Proposals: `check_job_response_limits` caps 10 per post and 5 active per freelancer.
- **Seller rules:**
  - 18+ to become a seller (`enforce_seller_type_age_gate`).
  - One capability only (`enforce_seller_capability`).
  - The type cannot change while a shop, freelancer profile or proposal exists (`enforce_seller_capability_switch`).
  - A seller phone can't be cleared (`enforce_seller_phone_not_cleared`).
  - A shop or service needs a contact phone (`enforce_seller_contact_phone`).
