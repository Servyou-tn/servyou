## Built as drawn (no decision needed)
- **Freelancer Card** (the Figma component, instanced 8 × on the page): 556 × 203 FIXED, 2 px `blue/600` stroke on every card, radius 12, pad 20 (the stroke is inside the 203: pad 18 + border 2), gap 16; avatar 72 `border/strong` holding an `Avatar` lg 56 — the picture, else the person glyph, **no initials**; name 16 SemiBold `blue/800`; title 14 muted in a FIXED 42 box (2 lines, reserved even when short); city row (pin 14 + 12 muted, gap 4); heart = the plain 20 outline icon (filled + danger when favourited, no circle, 44 px invisible hit area); chips row gap 8, pad 4/8, radius 6, `blue/50`, 12 Medium `blue/700`, **up to 4 real skills + « +N »** (2 + « +N » below `sm`, exactly what the 375 frame draws); footer space-between: « N services offerts » 13 muted (« N services » below `sm`, as drawn) and the primary `sm` Button, 120 × 32, radius 10, « Voir le profil ». The component is shared with /mes-favoris, so that page now shows the same card.
- **Filter Bar = the Services lens's `ServicesFilterBar`** (the same component the frame instances): search · Catégorie · Ville · **Prix** · Trier par. The old forked bar is deleted.
- **Prix is real**: a service counts when it is active, visible, in the chosen sector (if any) and its `starting_price_tnd` is inside [min, max] — the sector and the price describe the same service; a freelancer's price (for the range and for « Prix croissant / décroissant ») is the LOWEST starting price among those services (« à partir de »). Services without a price are ignored; a freelancer with none sorts last.
- **Grid** 2 columns, gap 24; gridWrap gap 32; shared `Pagination` with « Affichage X à Y sur N » (real N, thousands grouped in French as the frame draws « 1 248 »); 8 cards a page as drawn.
- **Toggle**: sunken track, text only, two links (82 / 98); « Bientôt » gone. `?vue=freelances` on `/marche/services` (the Boutiques lens pattern; independent of C2's `?type=`).
- **Visibility** (RLS-compatible, anon / authenticated client, no service-role): freelancers with at least one ACTIVE, non-moderated service, who are `is_published` and not `admin_hidden`; reads page past PostgREST's 1000-row limit; city compared trimmed.
- 375: toggle bar, then the cards at 343 / `FILL`, gap 16, pad 16 — and **no in-page filter**, because the frame draws none.

## OPEN QUESTIONS for the founder (nothing below is decided by me)
1. **1440 toggle state.** The frame draws « Services » as the selected segment on the Freelances page (the 375 frame has « Freelances » selected). I render the correct state (Freelances selected). Say if you want the literal frame.
2. **No filters at 375.** Built as drawn: a phone user cannot search / filter the Freelances lens (the shell header search goes to `/recherche`). Do you want the Services lens's bottom-sheet filter trigger here (one line to add), or is this the design?
3. **Mobile header.** The 375 frame's header is « Marketplace » (20 SemiBold) + a 343 × 41 sunken search — no hamburger, no logo, no language toggle. Our shell's mobile topbar is different on every page. This is app-wide shell work (mobile batch), not this page: I did not touch it.
4. **Shell chrome at 1440.** The frame's sidebar shows « Marketplace » as the single active item (no expanded Produits / Services children) and a signed-in Topbar (search placeholder « Rechercher une boutique, un produit… », bell, user). Shell — untouched; same question for the mobile batch.
5. **Filter Bar rhythm.** In the frame the search field (44 high, with an empty label row above and a helper row below) makes the bar block 97 high, controls vertically centred, 52 below the toggle. The shipped Services bar is 40 high at 16 below the toggle. I reused the shipped bar unchanged (it is « the same component » as the Services lens); matching the frame's spacing would change the Services lens too.
6. **Sort options.** The frame shows only « Trier par : Plus récents ». I kept the Services lens's three: Plus récents, Prix croissant, Prix décroissant (price = the freelancer's lowest starting price). The previous A→Z option is gone.
7. **/mes-favoris** now renders the new card (it is the same component). Please look at that page too.
8. **Mock data in the frame is inconsistent** (« TypeScript » in four cards although the skills differ, « +1 » and « +2 » side by side, « 8 services » vs « 8 services offerts »). It is data, not design: the chips show each freelancer's real skills (none of the 10 published profiles has any yet, so no chip row appears on live data — see the fixture comparison).
