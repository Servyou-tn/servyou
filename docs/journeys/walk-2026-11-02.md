# Journey walk — 2026-11-02 / 03 (localhost, production build)

Three journeys walked end to end on `next build` + `next start` (branch `docs/journey-walk-2026-11-02` = `origin/main`
6c0e17f, #296 included), FR then AR. **Report only — nothing was fixed.** 35 findings: 2 blocks · 17 confusing ·
16 cosmetic.

Rank: **blocks** = the journey cannot be completed · **confusing** = it can, but a user is likely to get lost or misled
· **cosmetic** = visual only. Screenshots: `docs/journeys/walk-2026-11-02/` (test data only; nothing with real names or
phone numbers).

## Top 10

1. **#1 blocks — a logged-out visitor cannot reach the marketplace from the home page** (FR and AR): every « browse »
   CTA and the 10 category tiles point back to `/`.
2. **#17 blocks — a freelancer is never asked for a phone**, yet the service and proposal success pages promise
   « contact sur WhatsApp ». The walked account finished every step with no number.
3. **#19 — a brand-new freelancer's dashboard shows « 4 consommateurs » and « 1 boutique »** that are not theirs.
4. **#31 — « Ajouter un produit » accepts a negative price** and shows « Le client paiera : -3 + 7 = 4 » with no error;
   no field ever says what is missing.
5. **#18 — « Votre profil est maintenant visible »** while the public page answers 404 until a service exists, and the
   « Voir mon profil public » button there is disabled as « bientôt disponible ».
6. **#33 — the user menu's « Voir mon profil public » opens the private « Mon compte »** for every role.
7. **#26 — French seller text scrambles in AR** (titles, descriptions, addresses): no `dir="auto"` isolation.
8. **#24 / #23 — service wizard:** « Ce qui est inclus » silently needs 3 lines, and the skills list covers « Suivant ».
9. **#7–#9 — the sign-in page after « Demander ce produit »** says nothing about why, has no way back, and
   « Créer un compte » drops the product (`?next=`).
10. **#14 / #16 — order actions:** « Confirmer » (dashboard) vs « Accepter » (orders) for one action, and the final
    « Confirmer la réception » runs on one click with no confirmation.

## Environment limits (not product bugs)

- **Images:** every Supabase image shows the grey placeholder and logs a 400 from `/_next/image` — this PC's DNS
  returns NAT64 addresses for Supabase, which Next 16's image optimizer refuses as private. Local only (known).
- **Turnstile:** `.env.local` has no Turnstile keys. A local `next start` then FAILS CLOSED on every protected submit
  (« La vérification de sécurité a échoué » on « Confirmer la demande ») — by design, see
  `src/lib/security/turnstile.ts`. The walk was rebuilt with Cloudflare's dummy keys from `.env.example`
  (`NEXT_PUBLIC_TURNSTILE_SITE_KEY` at build, `TURNSTILE_SECRET_KEY` at start; shell only, `.env.local` untouched).
- **Sign-in / sign-up forms:** Supabase Auth verifies their CAPTCHA token with the REAL secret, which a dummy token
  can never pass, so the forms cannot submit on localhost. The walk signs in through the cross-device reset link
  (`/nouveau-mot-de-passe?token_hash=…` → « Continuer », #294) instead. **Not walked: the real sign-in and sign-up
  submits, and the `/connexion?next=` return after a sign-in.** They need a check on the Vercel preview or production.
