# SERVYOU — FOUNDATION DOCUMENTS

This is the map of Servyou's foundation documents. Servyou is a Tunisia-native digital marketplace platform connecting consumers, freelancers, and e-commerce shop owners — built carefully, layer by layer, to the standard of serious institutions. The documents in this project are not afterthought README files. They are the deliberate, locked, source-of-truth specifications of what Servyou is, how it is built, and how it goes to market. When a hard decision arises that has no obvious answer, these documents are the references that resolve it.

The foundation has three tiers: the seven **Layer Documents** that define *what* Servyou is, the six **Pillar Documents** that define *how* Servyou is built and brought to market, and `CLAUDE.md` at the project root, which holds the *operational rules* that govern day-to-day engineering work. Together they are the bedrock under every feature, every pull request, every design decision, and every external conversation Servyou has with partners, evaluators, and the world.

## The Seven Layer Documents — What Servyou Is

The seven Layers were the first foundation built for Servyou, written section by section with the founder and locked before moving to the next. They define the platform from its soul outward: from the problem it solves and the vision it pursues, through the users it serves and the features they use, down into the data model, the security rules, the technical architecture, and the build plan that brings everything to life. Every Layer must remain consistent with every earlier one. The Layers are read in order on the first encounter and referenced individually thereafter.

**Layer 1 — Vision and Purpose.** The soul of Servyou: the core problem it solves (Tunisia's digital economy has no home of its own), the destination it pursues (by 2030, the trusted home Tunisians turn to in order to work, buy, and sell properly), the three core values (we protect our users, we are honest and transparent especially about money, we are genuinely Tunisian and not a copy of foreign platforms), the boundaries Servyou will not cross, and the success measures by which Servyou will know it is succeeding. When a hard decision arises with no obvious answer, Layer 1 is where to return. *File: `servyou-layer-1-vision.md`.*

**Layer 2 — Users and Roles.** Who uses Servyou and the rules that govern their roles: consumers (universal buying baseline, minimum age 16), freelancers (the digital-native young workforce, with UGC creators as a strategic emerging category, minimum age 18), e-commerce shop owners (including dropshippers, with Cash on Delivery as the Tunisian cultural payment default, minimum age 18), administrators (internal, separated cleanly from developer access for user-data protection), and the post-MVP role family of liberal professionals branching by profession. The locked role model — buying universal, one seller capability per user — shapes every later layer. *File: `servyou-layer-2-users-and-roles.md`.*

**Layer 3 — Features and Journeys.** What each user can actually do and the step-by-step journeys they take from arrival to outcome. Browsing open to everyone without account, signup at the moment of action, progressive data collection at every stage, the consumer journey from discovery to WhatsApp-coordinated COD, the shop owner's Converty-style dashboard, the freelancer's LinkedIn-meets-Instagram public profile, the job-posting reverse flow with its fairness limits, the administrator's moderation tools and aggregate-anonymous statistics, the cross-cutting features that bind the platform together, and the locked unified-platform growth strategy where social media is the marketing layer and Servyou is the transaction and organization hub. *File: `servyou-layer-3-features-and-journeys.md`.*

**Layer 4 — The Data Model.** The database structure that stores every user, shop, product, service, order, job post, favorite, and report, and how they connect. The universal `profiles` table with its optional `seller_type` capability, the shops and products tables built to support both stock-tracking sellers and dropshippers, the freelancer profiles and service listings with their media gallery, the unified `orders` table holding both product and service requests with COD delivery details, the job posts and responses with the fairness-limit constraints, the favorites and reports tables, and the categories taxonomy that serves products, services, and job posts in both French and Arabic. Every table traces back to a decision locked in Layers 1–3. *File: `servyou-layer-4-data-model.md`.*

**Layer 5 — Security and Permissions.** Who is allowed to see and change each piece of data, enforced at the database level via Row Level Security on every table. The three security layers of HTTPS, Supabase Auth, and RLS. The locked privacy-by-default principle with its user-initiated exception — even the platform's own administrator cannot read a user's private order content at will; access opens only when the user themselves attaches it to a report. This is a standard stricter than most platforms practice, and it is the operational expression of Layer 1's protect-users value. *File: `servyou-layer-5-security.md`.*

**Layer 6 — Technical Architecture.** The technology that builds Servyou: Next.js 16 with App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase (Postgres, Auth, automatic API, Row Level Security, Storage), Vercel for hosting and deployment, GitHub plus Git for source control. The deliberate minimalism — what Servyou explicitly does not add, and why. The image and file storage strategy. One clean, modern, professional stack, already live, built to grow only when real problems justify new pieces. *File: `servyou-layer-6-technical-architecture.md`.*

**Layer 7 — The Build Plan.** The order in which Servyou is built, from foundation to launched MVP. The ten phases — foundation and identity, the database skeleton, the shop owner side, the freelancer side, consumer discovery and requests, favorites, the job-posting system, multi-language French then Arabic, the admin dashboard with reports, and polish and launch. The cross-device and scalability commitments. The honest timeline and the pre-launch additions parked for Phase 10 (Sentry, Cloudflare Turnstile). *File: `servyou-layer-7-build-plan.md`.*

## The Six Pillar Documents — How Servyou Is Built and Brought to Market

The Pillars sit alongside the Layers and answer the second half of the question. The Layers define **what** Servyou is. The Pillars define **how** Servyou is built and brought to market — the engineering standards that govern the code, the design system that governs the visual identity, the brand and voice that govern the platform's personality, the target users we serve, the marketing strategy that grows us, and the roadmap that carries Servyou from MVP to the Tunisian national startup cup in 2027 and the world stage beyond.

The Pillars are built in the same disciplined fashion as the Layers — section by section with the founder, each section deliberated and locked before moving to the next. Pillars depend on each other: brand sits upstream of the design system (the visual identity expresses the brand); target users sit upstream of marketing strategy (you cannot write content without knowing who you are writing to); engineering standards sit upstream of everything technical.

**Pillar 1 — Engineering Standards and Architecture.** The standards that govern how Servyou is built at the code, security, architecture, quality, and compliance level. Section 1 — Anchor Standards — names the four external standards Servyou aligns to: the OWASP Top 10:2025 for application security, Tunisia's Loi organique 2004-63 plus GDPR Article 5 for data privacy, Google's Engineering Practices plus the Twelve-Factor App for code quality and architecture, and WCAG 2.2 Level AA for accessibility. The founding commitment is alignment from day one with measurable compliance as the documented trajectory toward launch and beyond. Sections 2 through 7 — coding standards in detail, security operations, architecture rules, quality discipline, external-integration standards, and compliance and legal posture — extend the anchor in subsequent sessions. *File: `pillar-1-engineering-standards.md`. Currently in the project: Section 1.*

**Pillar 2 — Design System and UX Principles.** Servyou's visual identity rules, design tokens, typography, color palette, component standards, spacing rhythm, iconography, and the accessibility constraints inherited from Pillar 1 Section 1.2.4 (WCAG 2.2 Level AA color contrast and touch-target minimums). The design system is the *visual expression* of Pillar 3's brand, so it builds on top of Pillar 3 rather than independent of it. *To be written.*

**Pillar 3 — Brand and Visual Identity.** Servyou's personality, voice, tone — the *feel* the platform projects. The words it uses and the words it never says. The relationship to Tunisian culture, the relationship to international professionalism. The soul of the design system, sitting upstream of Pillar 2. *To be written.*

**Pillar 4 — Target Users and Market.** The precise personas Servyou serves: names, ages, jobs, pains, where they live, how they find Servyou, what they expect. Anchored in real Tunisian market data and user research methodology. Sits upstream of Pillar 5 — marketing cannot be written without knowing who it speaks to. *To be written.*

**Pillar 5 — Content and Marketing Strategy.** How Servyou tells its story to the world. Social media, video content, launch plan, growth strategy, content calendar principles, channel selection. Anchored in how real marketing agencies build campaigns for clients. *To be written.*

**Pillar 6 — Roadmap.** The eighteen-month plan: MVP launch, traction milestones, the startup-label application from the Tunisian Ministry of Finance, the 2027 Tunisian national startup cup, the world cup stage in California. The concrete version of the founding ambition, with dates, measurable milestones, and decision points. Sits downstream of all the others because it sequences their delivery. *To be written.*

## `CLAUDE.md` — The Operational Rules

`CLAUDE.md` at the project root is the operational layer. Where the Layers define the platform and the Pillars define the standards, `CLAUDE.md` holds the day-to-day rules that govern engineering work — what to read before starting a task, how to verify before rebuilding, how Supabase errors must be surfaced, how Pull Requests are reviewed, how shipping discipline is held. It is the file every Claude Code session reads first, and the file that translates the high-level standards in Pillar 1 into operational discipline that touches every line of code. The "Engineering Discipline" section in `CLAUDE.md` is the operational expression of Pillar 1 Section 1's commitments; the "Parked for Phase 10" subsection holds the pre-launch additions (Sentry, Cloudflare Turnstile) that are not added prematurely.

`CLAUDE.md` is updated whenever a new operational rule earns its place through experience — typically after a session in which a specific class of mistake was caught and a specific discipline was identified to prevent it from recurring. The rules in `CLAUDE.md` are scar-earned, not invented.

## How to Read These Documents

For someone new to Servyou, the recommended reading order is: Layer 1 first (the vision and values that govern everything), then `CLAUDE.md` (the operational rules currently in force), then Pillar 1 (the engineering standards Servyou is built to), then Layers 2 through 7 in order (the platform specifics). The remaining Pillars are read as they are written. The whole foundation can be read in an afternoon by anyone who cares to understand what Servyou is.

For an existing reader returning to make a hard decision, the recommended path is to return to the most fundamental document that bears on the question. A decision about a feature returns to Layer 3. A decision about data returns to Layer 4. A decision about security or privacy returns to Layer 5 and Pillar 1 §1.2.2. A decision about whether to add a tool returns to Layer 6 and Pillar 1 §1.2.3. A decision about how to talk to a partner, an evaluator, or a judge returns to Pillar 1 Section 1 — the institutional credibility anchor — and to whichever Pillar speaks to the specific kind of conversation.

For a collaborator, partner, evaluator, or judge encountering Servyou for the first time, the recommended path is to read Layer 1 (to see the soul), Pillar 1 Section 1 (to see the engineering standard), and Layer 7 (to see the build plan and the trajectory). Those three documents together convey the seriousness of what Servyou is building, the standard it holds itself to, and the path it is on.
